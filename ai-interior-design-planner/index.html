FAQ

Common questions about AI-Assisted Interior Design Planner

Is the AI preview a final design?

No. It is an AI-assisted imagination preview only. Final dimensions, feasibility, materials, quotation and execution details are verified by AlterCraft's human design team.

Do I need exact dimensions?

Yes. Dimensions entered in the planner are treated as fixed site constraints. If a detail is missing, it should remain Not provided instead of being assumed.

Can AlterCraft execute the selected concept?

AlterCraft can review the selected imagination concept and then prepare a human-verified execution direction, measured quote, production plan and installation scope where feasible.
