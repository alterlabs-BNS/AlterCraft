FAQ

Common questions about Custom TV Unit Design in Ghaziabad

What details are needed for a TV unit quote?

Share wall width, TV size, socket position, router or set-top box location, storage need and one straight photo of the wall.

Can cables be hidden inside the TV unit?

Yes, but cable concealment should be planned before fabrication so access remains possible for repairs and upgrades.

Do you make TV units for small living rooms?

Yes. Floating consoles, shallow drawers and light paneling can keep compact rooms open while still giving storage.
